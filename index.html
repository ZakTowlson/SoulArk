Questions

Before you apply.

What's the difference between Brotherhood and the 1:1 programmes?

Brotherhood is the ongoing environment — brotherhood, mentorship access, events and accountability at £197/month. The Rebuild System and Leadership Protocol are private, structured 1:1 mentorships with Zak (and Nemanja for finances) that fast-track a specific transformation over 6 or 12 weeks. Both 1:1 programmes include lifetime Brotherhood access, so the environment continues long after the programme ends.

Is Soul Ark only for Christian men?

Yes. Faith is a core pillar of the framework, not an optional add-on. Soul Ark is built specifically for ambitious Christian men who want their faith, character, finances and leadership to grow together.

Do I need to already be disciplined to join?

No — the opposite. Soul Ark is built for men who know they're capable of more but haven't built the discipline yet. The environment, structure and brotherhood are what build that discipline. You don't need to arrive finished.

Why is membership application-based?

Soul Ark is a brotherhood, not an open marketplace. Every man is personally reviewed before entry to protect the standard of the men already inside. You cannot buy your way in — you apply, and you're accepted.

How much time does this actually take?

Brotherhood is built around your life — calls, resources and accountability fit around your schedule. The Rebuild System and Leadership Protocol require a genuine weekly commitment (a 90-minute call plus applied work) because real transformation isn't passive.

What if I can only start with Brotherhood?

That's exactly how the ecosystem is designed to work. Start in Brotherhood, build momentum inside the environment, and move into a 1:1 programme when you're ready to accelerate a specific area of your life.

What happens after I apply?

You'll be personally reviewed and, where it's a fit, invited to a short call before entry. This isn't a formality — it's how Soul Ark protects the standard of the brotherhood.
